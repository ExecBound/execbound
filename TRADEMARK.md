## You may, without asking

- **Name ExecBound accurately** to say that your software, service or work uses it, works with it, is compatible with it, or is based on it. For example: "a connector for ExecBound", "compatible with ExecBound", "based on ExecBound".
- **Pass on unmodified copies** of ExecBound under its own name, as this repository publishes them. A distribution's package of ExecBound may keep the name when its only changes are those needed to build and install it there, and security fixes ExecBound has published.
- **Keep the name inside your organization.** A version you run only for your own organization, changed or not, may keep ExecBound's name and its mark in the console, as long as you do not distribute it or offer it to others.
- **Name a package for ExecBound** in the form `yourname-for-execbound` or `yourname-execbound-connector`, beginning with a name of your own.
- **Write and speak about ExecBound,** in articles, books, talks, tutorials, reviews, courses and community events, as long as nothing presents your work as ExecBound LLC's or as approved by it. Honest criticism, comparison and review that name ExecBound are always allowed.
- **Use the mark, unaltered,** to link to this repository or to ExecBound's documentation at [docs.execbound.ai](https://docs.execbound.ai).

Where you use the name or the mark, you may add: "ExecBound is a trademark of ExecBound LLC."

## You may not, without written permission

- **Name your version ExecBound.** A modified version, a fork, a distribution, a product or a service may not be named "ExecBound", or anything confusingly similar, and may not use the mark as its own logo. Give it a name of its own; it may say that it is based on ExecBound.
- **Offer a service under the name.** A hosted or managed service may not be named ExecBound, or presented as ExecBound LLC's service.
- **Imply endorsement.** Do not suggest that ExecBound LLC endorses, certifies, sponsors or is affiliated with you, your work or your service, unless it is.

## When you distribute a modified version

Use your own name and logo for it, as above, and remove the mark from its interface. Keep the license and copyright notices the code carries. The name as it appears in the source code, in identifiers and in the commands, modules and packages the code itself uses, may stay, as long as your version is not presented to its users as ExecBound. You may describe it as based on ExecBound, and say what you changed.
