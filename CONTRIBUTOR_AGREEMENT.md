- **ExecBound** means ExecBound LLC.
- **You** means the individual who accepts this agreement or, when an organization accepts it under section 8, that organization and every entity that controls it, is controlled by it, or is under common control with it. **Control** means owning half or more of an entity's voting interests, or having the power to direct its management and policies, by contract or otherwise.
- **Contribution** means any original work of authorship, including any change to an existing work, that you intentionally submit to ExecBound for inclusion in its software or documentation. Submitting includes opening a pull request or an issue on a repository ExecBound maintains, and any other way of sending a work to ExecBound or its maintainers for that purpose. A work you mark in writing, when you submit it, as "Not a Contribution" is not one.
- **Work** means any software or documentation ExecBound makes available, to which a Contribution is submitted.
- **In writing** means on the pull request or issue concerned, or by email to justin.pitt@execbound.ai.

## 8. Accepting for an organization

An organization may accept this agreement for the people who contribute on its behalf. A person with authority to bind the organization emails justin.pitt@execbound.ai, beginning "[Organization] accepts the ExecBound contributor agreement, version 1.0, for the people listed below", and naming each person and the GitHub account they contribute from. The acceptance takes effect when ExecBound replies in writing to confirm the list it holds. The organization may change the list in the same way; removing a person leaves in place the licences already granted for their Contributions.

While a person is on the list, the Contributions they make for the organization are submitted by the organization under this agreement. They accept nothing personally for those Contributions; the pull request template has a box for saying the organization's acceptance covers them.

## 9. How you accept

You accept this agreement as an individual by ticking its box in the pull request template, or by writing on your pull request that you accept it. Ticking the box accepts the version the box names. Your acceptance covers the pull request you accept it on and every Contribution you submit afterwards, until you accept a later version. Code offered in an issue, a discussion or a review comment is used only once its author has accepted this agreement.

ExecBound is not obliged to use your Contributions. This agreement adds to the sign-off every commit carries under the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/); it does not replace it.

## 10. Publishing what ExecBound accepts

ExecBound will publish every Contribution it accepts under the licence of the files you submitted it to, AGPL-3.0-only or Apache-2.0. This binds anyone to whom ExecBound assigns this agreement.

## 11. Assignment

ExecBound may assign this agreement, and the licences it grants, to an affiliate or to a successor to its business.

## 12. Changes to this agreement

ExecBound may publish a new version of this agreement. It announces a new version in this repository, and the pull request template's box names the version it accepts. A new version applies only to Contributions you submit after you accept it; a Contribution an earlier version covers stays under that version.
